Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521) competition "One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge".

## What it does

`main.py` writes a single roast line (one line, under 200 characters) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output/entry.txt`).

The line roasts the phone in a single tight clause — its face unlock
recognises everyone except its owner — and the twist lands on the last words:

> My phone's face unlock opens for my brother and a photo of me — but not for me at 7 a.m.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
